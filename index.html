About EGY PACK

Precision film. Industrial strength.

EGY PACK is an Egyptian manufacturer of industrial stretch film, delivering wrapping solutions that protect goods from the production line to the final destination. Our multi-layer cast films combine exceptional stretch, cling and tear resistance to reduce film consumption while maximizing load security.

Product Range

Stretch Film Solutions

Every roll engineered for performance, consistency and cost efficiency.

Machine Stretch Film

High-performance film for automatic and semi-automatic pallet wrappers, up to 300% pre-stretch.

  • Width: 500 mm
  • Gauge: 17–35 µ
  • Core: 76 mm

Hand Stretch Film

Lightweight, easy-unwind rolls with excellent cling for manual wrapping operations.

  • Width: 450–500 mm
  • Gauge: 17–25 µ
  • Core: 50 mm

Pre-Stretched Film

Thin, ultra-strong film stretched at the factory — less effort, less material, same strength.

  • Gauge: 8–12 µ
  • Low tension
  • Eco-efficient

Colored & Custom Film

Black, blue and tinted films for identification and security, with custom sizes on demand.

  • UV-stabilized options
  • Opaque / tinted
  • Custom length

Manufacturing Technology

From Resin to Roll

  1. 01

    Premium Resins

    Selected LLDPE and metallocene grades blended for strength and elasticity.

  2. 02

    Cast Extrusion

    Multi-layer co-extrusion with precise chill-roll cooling for crystal clarity.

  3. 03

    Quality Control

    Continuous gauge monitoring and lab tests for stretch, puncture and cling.

  4. 04

    Winding & Packing

    Tension-controlled winding on robust cores, palletized and ready to ship.
